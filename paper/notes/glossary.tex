\section{Glossary}\label{notes:glossary}
This table defines the terms these notes use that the paper's glossary (Appendix~\paperref{app:glossary} of the paper) does not, and the paper's earlier vocabulary that moved text keeps.

{\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}P{4.4cm}P{12.1cm}@{}}
\toprule Term & Definition\\\midrule\endfirsthead
\toprule Term & Definition\\\midrule\endhead
\bottomrule\endlastfoot
Accepted length & Committed tokens per verify cycle, the bonus token included: $\tau$ pooled over cycles, $\bar\tau_{\rm req}$ averaged over requests as the DFlash model card does (Section~\ref{app:drafter-acceptance}).\\
Common mass $\alpha^\ast$ & The largest probability with which an exact sampler can emit a token before more is known, $\sum_i\inf_pp_i$ over the compatible laws (Proposition~\ref{prop:commonmass}).\\
Dead, deferred, soundly enclosed & The three classes of skipped work: no remaining consumer; a later consumer with a reconstruction path; or bounded well enough for every consumer that remains (Section~\ref{app:tail}).\\
Demand frontier & Suppression of head work after the first certified rejection in a chain (Proposition~\ref{prop:frontier}).\\
Emitted and materialized prefix & The tokens a cycle has returned and the tokens already consumed into every cache; the bonus token is emitted before it is materialized (Section~\ref{app:state}).\\
Envelope $\beta_i$ & The paper's ``error bound'': a proved bound on the error of the low-precision copy's score for vocabulary entry~$i$ (Theorem~\paperref{thm:envelope} of the paper).\\
Exact and lossy lever, exact and lossy stack & A lever is exact when every emitted token remains the target's decision under the stock-kernel contract, lossy otherwise; the lossy stack admits lossy levers under a quality budget declared before measuring (Sections~\ref{app:levers} and~\ref{app:stacks}).\\
First matrix, pinned matrix & The two sets of divergence runs: servers capped at 16 running requests with pools sized at start-up, and servers capped at 8 with identical pinned pools (the paper's unpinned and pinned runs, Appendix~\paperref{app:divergence} of the paper).\\
H1--H7, P1--P13, P-A to P-C & The programme's questions, the later proposals and three external drafting proposals (Table~\ref{tab:status}), all but P13 with a pre-registered kill test; P7a and P7b are the two parts of P7, and a proposal is listed once it has a result or a decision.\\
Kill test & A test fixed before a proposal is built, designed to reject it cheaply if its best case cannot pay; a pass does not support the proposal, it only lets it go on.\\
Layer-serial and overlapped ceilings & Throughput bounds under two execution models: per-request kernels after the weight GEMMs, as the engine runs, or overlapped with them (Equations~\eqref{eq:stack-floor} and~\eqref{eq:stack-floor-overlap}).\\
Replayed and snapshot verification & Two ways SGLang verifies a draft chain on the GDN layers: snapshot verification writes one FP32 state per draft token and copies the accepted one back at commit; replayed verification (\code{-{}-enable-linear-replayssm-spec}) keeps each draft token's inputs and folds the accepted prefix into the state at commit (Section~\ref{app:spec-state}).\\
Rounding flip, order flip, ambiguous & How a divergence reaches the token: BF16 rounding merges an unchanged accumulator order into a tie; the accumulator order reverses; or the order cannot be read from the data under the named error model (Section~\ref{notes:divergence}).\\
Rounding-preserving replay & Decoding that keeps a dense state anchor and the recent FP32 operands and replays them in the stock kernel's order, writing the state less often (P4, Section~\ref{app:minimal-state}).\\
Row & In moved text, either a vocabulary entry (a row of the head's weight matrix $W$) or a position (one head input of a batch); the paper keeps the two apart.\\
Second panel & The 80 prompts of the drafter's equality and acceptance runs: the first 16 chat, code and GSM8K training prompts of the serving workload's confirmation split, and 32 MATH-500 problems (Section~\ref{app:drafter-acceptance}).\\
Self-evidence & The low-precision copy of the head evaluated at the target's own head input, as opposed to transport from a drafter's summaries (Section~\paperref{sec:selfevidence} of the paper).\\
Survival $S(k)$, conditional acceptance $\alpha_k$ & The share of verify cycles that accept at least $k$ drafted tokens, and $S(k)/S(k-1)$ (Section~\ref{app:drafter-acceptance}).\\
Verify pass, draft step, cycle & The target's forward pass over a drafted block; one drafter forward; a draft, its verification and the state commit (Appendix~\paperref{app:profile} of the paper; Section~\ref{app:drafting}).\\
\end{longtable}}

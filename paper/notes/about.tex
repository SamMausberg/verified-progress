\section{About these notes}\label{notes:about}
These notes accompany the paper ``The Work a Verifier Needs'' (``the paper'' below). They hold the investigations made during the work that the paper's claims do not rely on: the programme's other questions and proposals with their tests and verdicts, the rest of the serving stack, drafting and repair, the stock engine's recurrent state under speculation, and further analysis of sampled decisions and transport. Most of the text was moved unchanged from the paper's appendices when the paper was edited for length; only cross-references changed, and a reference into the paper names it, as in ``Appendix~\paperref{app:divergence} of the paper''.

Moved text keeps the paper's earlier vocabulary, which differs from the paper's present terms as Table~\ref{tab:vocabulary} shows; Section~\ref{notes:glossary} defines the other terms these notes use.

Evidence is marked as in the paper. A committed result cites its entry in the register of these notes (Section~\ref{notes:register}), printed as [N-E$n$], and a result that was not measured is said to be so where it would appear. The paper numbers its own entries without the prefix, E$n$, so an ID never means two things. The two registers are numbered separately: an entry that both documents cite lists the same files in both but usually has a different number, so N-E$n$ and the paper's E$n$ for the same files differ; the paper's numbers are in its register (Appendix~\paperref{app:register} of the paper). Oracle bounds, derived estimates and single runs are labelled as such where they are reported, and a refuted proposal stays with the mechanism that refuted it.

The notes build from the repository's \path{paper/} directory after the paper, with \code{latexmk -pdf notes/research\_notes.tex}.

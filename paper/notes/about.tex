\section{About these notes}\label{notes:about}
These notes accompany the paper ``The Work a Verifier Needs'' (``the paper'' below). They hold the investigations made during the work that support none of the paper's claims: the programme's other questions and proposals with their tests and verdicts, the rest of the serving stack, drafting and repair, the stock engine's recurrent state under speculation, and further analysis of sampled decisions and transport. Most of the text was moved unchanged from the paper's appendices when the paper was edited for length; only cross-references changed, and a reference into the paper names it, as in ``Appendix~\paperref{app:divergence} of the paper''.

Moved text keeps the paper's earlier vocabulary. \emph{Self-evidence} is the int8 (or other low-precision) copy of the head evaluated at the target's own head input; an \emph{envelope} is the proved error bound $\beta_i$ on that copy's score of vocabulary entry~$i$; and a \emph{row} is either a vocabulary entry (a row of the head's weight matrix $W$) or a position (one head input of a batch), as the context says. Section~\ref{notes:glossary} defines the other terms these notes use.

Evidence is marked as in the paper. A committed result cites its entry in the register of these notes (Section~\ref{notes:register}), printed as [E$n$]; a designed result without a committed file carries [pending D$n$], and the pending list says where its evidence is to be committed. The paper and the notes number their entries separately, and an entry that both cite appears in both registers with the same files. Oracle bounds, derived estimates and single runs are labelled as such where they are reported, and a refuted proposal stays with the mechanism that refuted it.

The notes build from the repository's \path{paper/} directory after the paper, with \code{latexmk -pdf notes/research\_notes.tex}.

\term{main}{Head input $h$}{The BF16 vector the engine feeds to the output head (the final norm's output).}
\term{main}{Real logit $z_i$}{$\langle w_i,h\rangle$ in exact arithmetic over the stored BF16 operands.}
\term{main}{Stock head}{SGLang's head at the pin: a cuBLAS BF16 GEMM with FP32 accumulation and BF16 output, then \code{torch.argmax} (first maximal index).}
\term{main}{Batch shape $M$}{The number of head inputs in one head GEMM call; cuBLAS chooses its kernel by it.}
\term{main}{\Rreal, \Rbf, \Rstock, \Srace}{The four references for one decision (Definition~\ref{def:contracts}).}
\term{main}{Error model $\gamma$, $G_i$}{An assumed bound $G_i=\gamma\sum_j|w_{ij}h_j|$ on the stock kernel's accumulation error (Table~\ref{tab:assumptions}).}
\term{main}{Envelope $\beta_i$}{A proved bound on the error of the low-precision copy's value for row $i$.}
\term{main}{Candidate}{A row the level-0 screen cannot exclude; it is re-scored from its BF16 row.}
\term{main}{Fallback}{The stock head on the same inputs at the same batch shape; whole-batch or row-wise.}
\term{main}{Self-evidence; transport}{Enclosures from a low-precision copy of the head at the target's own input; enclosures carried from a drafter's per-tile summaries to it.}
\term{main}{$\rho$; $\delta^\ast_i$}{Drift ratio $\norm{h^t-h^d}_2/\norm{h^t}_2$ of a draft pair; the $\rho$ below which transport beats int8 on row $i$.}
\term{main}{Held-out}{From prompts that no choice of head, envelope or threshold was fitted on.}

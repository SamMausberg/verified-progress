\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}\label{sec:prior}
Descriptions follow our literature review \ev{litreview}; numbers quoted from other work are the authors' own.

\paragraph{Certified low-precision heads}\label{sec:prior-certified} The greedy form of the certified head is public several times over. sparkpipe screens with an E4M3 shadow whose per-group certificate covers CUDA-core FMA accumulation and re-scores survivors exactly, for decode and MTP argmax rows of a model with the same 248,320-row vocabulary~\cite{sparkpipehead}. dgpp bounds logits from the high bit planes of an int8 head and takes the quantized head as its reference~\cite{dgpphead}; a Metal implementation re-scores with a replica of the stock kernel~\cite{lagunaprune}; knlp reports an argmax match of 0.998 at 14B parameters~\cite{knlphead}. Du et al.\ show why the reference must be named: BF16 and FP16 greedy decoding diverge on 49--100\% of prompts~\cite{precisioninvariant}.

\paragraph{Screening and sampling} Cheap scores with bounds and exact refinement are old in similarity search~\cite{vafile,fexipro,lemp,ramgray2012}, where dense multiplication often wins end to end~\cite{maximus}. SVD-Softmax and HiRE recompute a fixed top set with no certificate~\cite{svdsoftmax,hire}; CSV-Decode certifies top-$k$ with cluster bounds $\langle\mu_c,h\rangle+R_c\norm h$~\cite{csvdecode}, the static screen of Table~\ref{tab:transport}, from which transport differs only in starting at the draft state. A$^\ast$ Sampling and Mussmann et al.\ sample exactly from bounded scores~\cite{astar,mussmann2017}; FlashSampling fuses Gumbel-max sampling into the head over every tile, exact in distribution with tiling-dependent noise~\cite{flashsampling}. Batch-invariant kernels make outputs reproducible across batches~\cite{he2025nondeterminism,sglangdeterministic}, which changes the kernel a certificate must match.

\paragraph{Speculative decoding} Drafter-invariant decoding couples draft and target through shared Gumbel noise~\cite{daliri2025,rowan2025}, which Section~\ref{sec:decisions} uses to make sampled verification a race; DSpark and D-cut schedule verification depth and state the non-anticipating condition for lossless sampled speculation~\cite{dspark,dcut}. Drafters such as EAGLE, native MTP and DFlash project through a head at every draft step~\cite{eagle,gloeckle2024,dflashpaper}, and draft-vocabulary reduction trades their acceptance for draft cost~\cite{frspec,vocabtrim,specvocab}; a certified head keeps the full-vocabulary token and also applies to the target.

\paragraph{What is and is not new} Norm or quantization bounds, bound-then-refine search, certified greedy screening of an LM head, sampling from bounded scores, drafter-invariant verification and the non-anticipating condition are prior art. For the certified head we claim only an envelope sound for tensor-core accumulation (for a kernel that computes the modelled arithmetic), a contract against a closed-source stock kernel with that kernel as the fallback, the extension to seeded sampling and fixed-noise verification, and measurements on the engine's own states. Transport of draft-side tile summaries is the one mechanism here without a precedent we found, and it is a negative result.

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}\label{sec:prior}
\paragraph{Certified low-precision heads}\label{sec:prior-certified} Sparkpipe screens with an E4M3 copy whose per-group certificate covers CUDA-core FMA accumulation and recomputes the survivors exactly, for a model with the same vocabulary~\cite{sparkpipehead}, and dgpp bounds logits from the high bit planes of an int8 head, with the quantized head as reference~\cite{dgpphead}. A Metal implementation recomputes its candidates with a replica of its engine's kernel~\cite{lagunaprune}, and knlp reports argmax matches of 0.998--1.000~\cite{knlphead}. Du et al.\ show why the reference must be named: BF16 and FP16 greedy decoding diverge on 49--100\% of prompts~\cite{precisioninvariant}.

\paragraph{Screening and sampling} Bound-and-refine search is old in similarity search~\cite{vafile,fexipro,lemp,ramgray2012}, where dense multiplication often wins end to end~\cite{maximus}. SVD-Softmax and HiRE recompute a fixed top set, uncertified~\cite{svdsoftmax,hire}. CSV-Decode certifies top-$k$ with cluster bounds~\cite{csvdecode}, the static screen of Section~\ref{sec:geometry}; transport differs only in starting at the draft's head input. FlashSampling fuses Gumbel-max sampling, exact in distribution, into the head over every tile~\cite{flashsampling}, with tiling-dependent noise in its public kernel~\cite{flashsamplingcode}. Batch-invariant kernels make outputs batch-independent~\cite{he2025nondeterminism,sglangdeterministic} but change the kernel a certificate must match.

\paragraph{Speculative decoding} Drafter-invariant decoding couples draft and target by shared Gumbel noise~\cite{daliri2025,rowan2025}, which Section~\ref{sec:decisions} uses to make sampled verification a race, free of the admission bias DSpark and D-cut describe (\citealp{dspark,dcut}; Appendix~\ref{app:stopping}). EAGLE, native MTP and DFlash drafters project through a head at each draft step~\cite{eagle,gloeckle2024,dflashpaper}, and draft-vocabulary reduction trades acceptance for draft cost~\cite{frspec,vocabtrim,specvocab}. A certified head keeps the full vocabulary and serves the target.

\paragraph{What is and is not new} Norm or quantization bounds, bound-then-refine search, certified greedy screening of an LM head, sampling from bounded scores and drafter-invariant verification are prior art. What is new is the contract with a closed-source kernel's rounding (Section~\ref{sec:contributions}); transport of draft-side tile summaries is the one mechanism without a precedent we found, and it is a negative result.

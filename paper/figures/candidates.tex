% Figure fig:candidates: what the certified head costs on real positions.
% (a) Measured: evidence/head_geometry/selfevidence_plain4b_ccdf.csv, the share of the 6,005
%     held-out plain-decode positions that need at least k candidates, per copy of the head.
% (b) Lines derived: 1-(1-p)^M with p the held-out per-position fallback shares under each error
%     model (figures/data/json_values.csv, copied from evidence/head_geometry/rstock_plain4b.json).
%     Markers measured: the GPU kernel's share of groups of M consecutive positions of the first
%     60,000 capture rows that need a fallback (evidence/certified_head/head_path_time.csv,
%     batch_fallback_rate and hopper_batch_fallback_rate); the shares printed at M = 128 are
%     these measured values, read from the same file.
% (c) evidence/certified_head/head_path_time.csv, a microbenchmark of the head alone with probes
%     on: time per call over the stock head and argmax (stock_us). The decided batch (w8a16_us,
%     median over 30 graph replays; whiskers w8a16_p10_us, w8a16_p90_us) is measured; the other two
%     series are expected times under the conservative model, computed from measured times and the
%     measured fallback rates of (b): per-position fallback (columns_expected_us) and whole-batch
%     fallback (w8a16_expected_us).
% In (b) and (c), batches above M = 16 are consecutive capture rows, not engine batches: the
% capture's engine batches held at most 16 requests (evidence/certified_head/README.md). Both
% panels shade that region and the caption explains the shading once.
% Layout (style.tex): equal plot heights; (a) is wider for its six decades. (a)'s legend sits in its
% empty lower right, below the int4 curve's last segment (which reaches 0.13% only at k = V);
% the FP8 label sits right of its curve near k = 64 and the int4 label under its curve's right
% part. (b)'s legend is drawn from \addlegendimage samples (line and mark together) with the
% status split in its header; its curves and markers carry no legend entries. All titles centred.

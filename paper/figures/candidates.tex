% Figure fig:candidates: what the certified head costs on real positions.
% (a) Measured: evidence/head_geometry/selfevidence_plain4b_ccdf.csv, the share of the 6,005
%     held-out plain-decode positions that need at least k candidates, per copy of the head.
% (b) Lines derived: 1-(1-p)^M with p the held-out per-position fallback shares under each error
%     model (figures/data/json_values.csv, copied from evidence/head_geometry/rstock_plain4b.json);
%     the printed shares at M = 128 are computed from them. Markers measured: the GPU kernel's
%     share of batches of M consecutive rows of the first 60,000 capture rows that need a
%     fallback (evidence/certified_head/head_path_time.csv, batch_fallback_rate and
%     hopper_batch_fallback_rate).
% (c) evidence/certified_head/head_path_time.csv, a microbenchmark of the head alone with probes
%     on: time per call over the stock head and argmax (stock_us). Decided batch (w8a16_us,
%     whiskers w8a16_p10_us and w8a16_p90_us) is measured; the other series are expected times
%     computed from measured times and the measured fallback rates of (b): whole-batch fallback
%     under each model (w8a16_expected_us, hopper_w8a16_expected_us) and per-position fallback
%     under the conservative model (columns_expected_us).
% In (b) and (c), batches above M = 16 are consecutive capture rows, not engine batches: the
% capture's engine batches held at most 16 requests (evidence/certified_head/README.md). Both
% panels shade that region; (b) names it, and (c)'s legend leaves no room to repeat the label.
\vpjson{fallback_share_hopper}{\phop}
\vpjson{fallback_share_conservative}{\pcons}
\pgfmathsetmacro{\fbhop}{100*(1-(1-\phop)^128)}
\pgfmathsetmacro{\fbcons}{100*(1-(1-\pcons)^128)}
